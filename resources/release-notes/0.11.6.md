## Features

- **Custom skins** — turn any local image into a full Peng theme. Settings → Appearance → Custom Skins opens a two-pane studio: pick a PNG/JPEG/WebP, and the image is analyzed on your machine to derive matching light and dark palettes, with a contrast gate that blocks unreadable combinations before you can save. Tune the background focal point, safe-side scrim, work-page intensity, panel opacity (20–100%), panel blur (0–24px), and whether the empty-session wordmark fades out after its intro animation. Skins follow your system/light/dark preference, live in `~/.craft-agent/themes/`, and can be applied per app or per workspace. **Images never leave your computer.** See `docs/user_manual/custom-skins.md` for the full guide.

## Improvements

- Usage-quota lookups now report the specific reason they failed instead of always showing a generic "unavailable" message.
- Observability (traces and insights) is enabled by default after passing its verification gate.
- Image-processing failures now distinguish "the local image component is unavailable" from "this image is corrupt", so the suggested fix matches the actual problem.

## Bug Fixes

- Fixed image processing in packaged builds, where the sidecar could not load the native image component; it now runs in a separate process.
- Scenic and skin glass styling no longer rewrites floating overlays, and the scenic glass base color is derived from the active theme instead of being hard-coded to a dark value.

## Breaking Changes

- None. All changes are backward-compatible.
